# 2. Proposed High-Level Architecture

This diagram illustrates the decoupling of monolithic prompt strings into standalone `.md` files managed by `prompt_loader.py`, alongside the 2-phase summary synthesis engine.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    subgraph PromptsRepo ["Decoupled Prompts Layer (/prompts)"]
        P0["0.common_instructions.md\n(Global JSON Rules & Confidence Scale)"]
        P1["1.classifier_pass1_system.md"]
        P2["2.classifier_pass2_system.md"]
        P3["3.teams_chat.md"]
        P4["4.teams_meeting.md"]
        P5["5.page_classifier.md"]
        P6["6.extraction.md"]
        P7["7.dimension_rescue.md"]
        P8["8.unit_reconciler.md"]
        PL["prompt_loader.py\n(Dynamic Stitched Prompt Injector)"]
        
        P0 --> PL
        P1 & P2 & P3 & P4 & P5 & P6 & P7 & P8 --> PL
    end

    subgraph ServiceBus ["Azure Service Bus Workers (/listener)"]
        W1["worker.py (Email)"]
        W2["extraction_worker.py (CAD)"]
        W3["teams_worker.py (Chat & Meetings)"]
        W4["rules_worker.py (Heuristics)"]
    end

    subgraph ExecutionEngines ["Execution Engines"]
        E1["classifier/classifier.py\n(Two-Pass Lead Triage)"]
        E2["new_app/fanout_router.py\n(Async Drawing Extraction)"]
        E3["extraction/summary_enricher.py\n(Deterministic Scope Synthesizer)"]
    end

    PL --> E1 & E2 & W3
    W1 --> E1
    W2 --> E2
    E2 --> E3
    E1 -.->|Phase 1 Summary| E3
```
